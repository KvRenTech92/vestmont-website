Engagement Models

Three ways to work with us.

01

Project-Based

Fixed-scope engagements for a specific deal — feasibility, capital raise, or exit. Defined deliverables, defined fee.

02

Retainer

Ongoing advisory for active sponsors. Monthly retainer, unlimited deal screening, capital stack design, and lender introductions.

03

Co-Development

Selective JV arrangements where we contribute capital strategy, lender relationships, and deal execution alongside sponsor equity.

Case Study

Life Storage · Gilbert, AZ

A 910-unit, 125,000 SF self-storage facility with 37 covered RV spaces — $30M ground-up development for Sovereign Group of Companies.

Vestmont performed the initial underwriting, led pre-development through zoning and entitlement, structured the financing, managed the full development cycle, onboarded operations, and executed the disposition.

01

Initial Underwriting

Market analysis, feasibility study, and proforma development. Validated demand drivers, competitive supply, and rent assumptions before capital was committed.

02

Pre-Development

Site selection, zoning and entitlement, architectural coordination, vendor selection, and GC procurement. Managed the full permitting timeline from concept through building permit.

03

Development & Financing

Structured the capital stack, placed construction financing, and oversaw the build — budget tracking, draw management, and schedule accountability through CO.

04

Onboarding & Stabilization

Lease-up strategy, property management onboarding, operational systems setup, and financial reporting through stabilization targets.

05

Disposition

Marketed and sold the stabilized asset at $30.5M — full-cycle execution from dirt to disposition.
